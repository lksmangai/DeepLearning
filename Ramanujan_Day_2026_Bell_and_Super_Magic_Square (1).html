The Ramanujan Lakshmana Christmas Bell

A bell cast from square roots instead of bronze. Every line below is exactly 2027, the year it rings in.

Year to welcome 2027

Each line opens the last number of the line above into a new square root, shown in gold. The bell never ends, and it never stops equalling 2027.

Why every line is the same number

Srinivasa Ramanujan loved square roots hidden inside square roots. This bell uses the same idea, started from a rule every school student meets.

  1. Start with the difference of two squares

    a² − b² = (a + b) × (a − b)

  2. Put in a = 2027 and b = 1000

    2027² = 1000² + 1027 × 3027

    1,000,000+3,108,729=4,108,729=2027²

  3. Do the same to the last number, again and again

    In general, with m = 1000 and x = 27: m·n + x = √( m² + (m(n−1) + x) × (m(n+1) + x) ), beginning at n = 2. Choose m = 1 and x = 0 and the same recipe returns Ramanujan’s own 3 = √(1 + 2√(1 + 3√(1 + 4√…))).

What if you cut the bell short?

Stop after a number of square roots and put a plain 1 where the rest should go. The answer is wrong at first, then closes in on 2027.

A Super Magic Square for Christmas Eve, 24-12-2026

Ramanujan built a magic square with his birth date across the top row. Rows, columns, diagonals, the four corners and the 2×2 boxes all add up to 139. All but two: the middle boxes at the top and the bottom give 56 and 222.

The Ramanujan Lakshmana Super Magic Square closes that gap. Write a date as day, month, century, year and set bb = (dd + mm − cc + yy) ÷ 2 in the third row. Now all nine boxes agree with everything else.

Why Christmas Eve and not 22 December?

Not every date has a Super Magic Square. The four numbers of the date must add up to an even number, and no number may appear twice outside the top row. In 2026 Ramanujan’s own birthday just misses.

  • 22-12-2026No. bb = (22 + 12 − 20 + 26) ÷ 2 = 20, which repeats the century, 20.
  • 23-12-2026No. 23 + 12 + 20 + 26 = 81 is odd, so bb is not a whole number.
  • 24-12-2026Yes. bb = 21, and all 20 sums come to 82.
